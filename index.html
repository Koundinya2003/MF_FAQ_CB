About FundBot

What is FundBot

FundBot is a facts-only FAQ assistant for Mirae Asset mutual fund schemes. It is built as part of the LIP Challenge — Mutual Fund FAQs milestone.

It helps first-time investors find clear, sourced answers to common questions about mutual funds — without jargon, opinions, or investment advice.
