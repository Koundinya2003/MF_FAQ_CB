Weights fold into term frequencies before scoring. Bigrams are indexed next to single words. Fund affinity boosts the scheme the question names and suppresses the other three, so a midcap question never gets the large cap figure.

Why no embeddings? sentence transformers pulls in about 2 GB of torch. That does not fit a free serverless tier, and cold starts would take tens of seconds.

Confidence = TF IDF cosine × IDF coverage
"What is the capital of Mongolia" matched the capital gains document at 0.29 on cosine alone. Coverage asks how much of the question's IDF mass a document explains, and charges unseen words like Mongolia at maximum IDF. That pushes them under the floor.

0.06 was picked from a sweep: it answers 97.6% of real questions with zero false answers.

Built free, with its limits written down

06 · STACK AND LIMITS
Free tooling end to end
  • VercelStatic frontend in public/ plus Python serverless functions in api/
  • OpenRouterFree DeepSeek model for rephrasing. Optional.
  • Python stdlibTokenizer, synonyms, BM25F and TF IDF. No vector database.
  • FlaskOne app behind /api/ask, /api/health and a debug /api/search
  • CIpytest plus the retrieval quality gate on every push
  • DockerFor Fly.io, Render, Hugging Face Spaces or a VPS
Known limits
  • point in timeSources as of June 2025, no live data. Expense ratios change monthly, so some answers link the TER page instead.
  • four schemesOther Mirae funds get "I don't know". Other fund houses are refused.
  • lexical ceilingA paraphrase with no shared vocabulary will miss. The fix is more phrasings in questions.
  • no memoryEach question stands alone, so "and for the midcap?" will not resolve.
